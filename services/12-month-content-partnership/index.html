Main Ongoing Offer • Brighton & UK B2B

A 12-month content partnership built around a founder’s real calendar

Most B2B teams know video and LinkedIn matter, but consistency breaks when filming competes with running the business. We build a steady 12-month rhythm where scope and cadence are agreed in discovery.

1. Discovery & Audience Fit

We map what your business is building, who needs to see it, and how much time you can realistically commit each month.

2. Batch Planning & Prep

Topics, talking points and shot lists are prepared in advance so filming days run calmly without guesswork.

3. One Shoot → Many Formats

One recording session produces LinkedIn vertical clips, longer YouTube videos, website loops and editorial photography.

4. Approvals, Delivery & Review

Clear review cuts, two included revision rounds, ready-to-publish masters, and quarterly reviews to sharpen what performs.
